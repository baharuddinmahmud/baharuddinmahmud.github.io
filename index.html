Featured Projects

🏥 Medical Vial Identification System (HoloLens 2)

Led development of mixed reality system for accurate medical vial identification using deep learning and Microsoft HoloLens 2. Funded by Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) through AHRQ.

  • Real-time object detection and classification in augmented reality
  • Custom deep learning model optimized for HoloLens 2 constraints
  • Deployed in medical environments for look-alike medication safety
  • Published: Future Internet (2025) and Electronics (2024)

🔬 3D Volumetric Image Segmentation

Deep learning-based segmentation of 3D volumetric images for microstructural analysis with applications in medical imaging and material science.

  • Advanced 3D CNN architecture for volumetric medical data
  • Efficient processing of CT/MRI scans
  • Applications in surgical planning and material characterization
  • Published: Sensors (2023)

🌾 AgriNet-Light: Agricultural Disease Detection

Lightweight deep learning model for real-time agricultural disease detection and plant pathology classification.

  • Optimized for mobile and edge device deployment
  • Fast, accurate mango leaf disease classification
  • Practical solution for farmers and agricultural professionals
  • Published: Emerging Science Journal (2024) & Springer Nature book chapter (Accepted)
